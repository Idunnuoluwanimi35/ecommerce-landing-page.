Use `.jpg`, `.png`, or `.webp` images and keep the filename (or update its `src` in `index.html`). Product and category images should be well-lit and cropped around the item. The hero image works best with a transparent background or a wide crop. The current product names, prices, and discounts are sample content; confirm the actual catalogue and currency before using them for sales.
